PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Shouldertap is private, invite-only communication for friends, family, partners and colleagues who want a short message acknowledged. The free iPhone companion sends taps to known recipients and can link to an existing Mac inbox to receive and answer taps. There is no public feed, discovery, advertising or username/password signup.

ACCESS AND MAIN FEATURES
Install Shouldertap for Mac from https://shouldertap.app/download (macOS 14+), open it and create a fresh inbox. Setup starts a seven-day trial; no purchase or pre-issued login is needed.
To send: choose Invite someone on the Mac. On iPhone choose I have an invite, scan the QR or paste its link, choose a name/color and pair. Send a tap; answer On it, In 10 min or with text on the Mac. The answer appears in the iPhone Tap tab while open.
To receive: on the Mac choose Add a Mac or iPhone. On iPhone choose Link to my Mac (or the Inbox tab), then scan/paste the link code. Allow notifications. Send a tap to that inbox from another paired sender/device. The linked iPhone shows an in-app tap, a Live Activity on supported devices/settings, or a notification fallback. Answer on the iPhone; confirm the sender receives the answer and the tap dismisses on linked devices. Inbox also manages senders, invites and devices. Codes are one-use and generated during setup.
Help and privacy > Try a sample tap is a clearly labeled local explanation; it does not send a real message and does not replace live testing. Please contact support if assisted review access or a trial extension is needed.

USER CONTENT, BLOCKING AND DELETION
Only invited senders can contact an inbox. In Inbox, remove a sender to revoke/block their access; a new invite is needed to reconnect. A sender can unpair. Help and privacy > Report a problem or abuse prepares an email/share report; long-press a recent sent tap to report its tap ID. Reports are sent only by the user's action in their mail/share app. Support handles reports; the server filters a limited set of abusive/threatening phrases.
Help and privacy > Delete my data with [person] deletes that sender pairing, name, messages and replies from the live server and phone. Repeat for each pairing; other senders are unaffected. Use a disposable pairing for deletion testing. Inbox device removal unlinks that device; it is not deletion of the entire Mac inbox. Inbox deletion is managed from the Mac companion.

EXTERNAL SERVICES AND PAYMENT
Cloudflare hosts the HTTPS/WebSocket backend and stores inbox, pairing and message data using Workers/Durable Objects. Apple APNs delivers incoming tap notifications and Live Activity updates; device/activity tokens are stored for delivery. Device credentials are kept in the iPhone Keychain. Creem handles the separate Mac/web inbox purchase. The iPhone app has no purchase flow, checkout link, price or prompt to buy. Sending pauses when the recipient trial ends. The free companion model is submitted for assessment under 3.1.3(f). No AI services or third-party content feeds are used.

REGIONS AND RIGHTS
Initial App Store availability is U.S. only. App functionality has no region-specific variants. This is everyday communication, not a regulated service or an emergency service; it contains no licensed third-party catalog.
