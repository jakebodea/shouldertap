## Description

A little tap. A clear answer.

Shouldertap helps you reach the people you know and get a reply. Create an inbox on your iPhone, invite someone, and let them send you a short message.

HARD TO MISS Receive taps in the app, on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island on supported iPhones. Add Shouldertap for Mac to show taps across your connected displays, even over full-screen apps.

CLOSE THE LOOP Answer “On it,” “In 10 min,” or with a few words. The sender sees your reply, and answering clears the tap from your linked devices.

YOUR PEOPLE, BY INVITATION Share an invite link or QR code. Invited people can send from iPhone or a browser. You choose who can reach you and can remove a sender whenever you need to.

START ON YOUR PHONE Create your inbox, invite people and manage your devices from iPhone. Connect a Mac whenever you want taps on your computer too.

Core messaging is free. An internet connection is required. Notification and Live Activity delivery depend on device support, permissions and system settings. Replies to taps you send update while the app is open; they do not trigger push notifications.

Shouldertap is for everyday communication, not emergencies.
